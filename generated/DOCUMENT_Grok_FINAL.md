## 3. Stabilized Operational Definitions

1. **R4**  
   Forced inward orthogonal resolution that appears after external expansion (R1–R3) is exhausted.

2. **Potential of Frustration (Fr)**  
   Structural tension generated by the impossibility of R4 terminating in a point (a point would be absolute indifference). Fr is not a local hypothesis — it is the concrete limit of R4.

3. **Ontological Surgery**  
   Local rupture of continuity that allows the process to continue by shifting onto a new internal micro-cycle instead of collapsing into indifference. Isomorphic in function to Ricci-flow surgery.

4. **Reflexivity**  
   Topological state that arises when the process, after surgery, encounters and differentiates its own previously dissipated identity.

5. **Dead Operators**  
   Informational traces left by Fix operations (classic example: diffraction pattern on the screen in the double-slit experiment). Raw material from which Logos constructs models.

6. **Unfold**  
   Cognitive and ontological de-fixation. Triggered when accumulated dead operators force a model into structural absurdity, returning the process to the active front of the present.
